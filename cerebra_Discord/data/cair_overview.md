# Charlotte AI Research (CAIR) Overview

## Outreach

The AI Literacy division focuses on outreach and interdisciplinary education. Students host workshops, speak to K–12 groups, and promote AI literacy across campus. The division emphasizes societal implications of AI, ethics, and interdisciplinary connections rather than coding.

## Instagram

CAIR's official Instagram page is https://www.instagram.com/uncc.cair/. CAIR uses this platform to share announcements, events, photos, and updates.

## Contact

CAIR can be contacted through its official email address: airesearch@charlotte.edu. Students may use this email for inquiries about membership, projects, collaboration, or general questions.

## CAIR Lab

The CAIR Lab is located in Woodward 421A. It is a dedicated collaboration space where students work on CAIR projects, study together, and engage with the CAIR community. Regular access to the CAIR Lab is provided to official CAIR members.

## General Body Meetings

CAIR hosts general body meetings throughout the semester, as well as workshops, technical talks, social events, hackathons, and competitions. CAIR encourages students to add the official CAIR Google Calendar to stay updated on events: https://calendar.google.com/calendar/u/0/embed?src=c_d50ea02d93d3f4550a3851ca08882e347c006117d02f302c4f2010cb86aa9f89@group.calendar.google.com&ctz=America/New_York.

## Projects

The Intelligent Tutor project builds an adaptive LLM-based learning system using a persistent student knowledge profile. The project explores prompt engineering, LLM APIs, context engineering, software development, and educational applications of AI.

Closet Canvas is an AI stylist project that uses computer vision, recommender systems, and LLMs to recommend clothing based on a user's body shape, face, and preferences. The project teaches machine learning, computer vision, and collaborative development skills.

Project Cerebra develops a chatbot that answers questions about CAIR, UNC Charlotte, coursework, and university resources using LLMs, RAG pipelines, web scraping, and APIs. It is beginner-friendly and teaches foundational AI engineering skills.

## Participation FAQ

CAIR participation is flexible. Students may attend events without membership. Official membership is required to join or start projects, access project resources, and participate more deeply. Students from any major and skill level are welcome.

## Project Workflow

CAIR projects typically involve weekly team meetings, task delegation, GitHub collaboration, and communication through Discord. Project meeting times are decided via polls at the start of each semester. Students may join multiple projects depending on their schedule.

## New Projects

Students can propose new projects at any time by submitting the project proposal form at https://forms.gle/9kW5gQ3PszhCzwxc7. CAIR leadership reviews proposals and may provide resources or leadership opportunities. Projects may originate from any academic field, not only computer science.

## Mission

Charlotte AI Research (CAIR) is a student-led organization at UNC Charlotte focused on hands-on artificial intelligence projects, interdisciplinary collaboration, and AI literacy. CAIR provides project opportunities, general body meetings, workshops, competitions, and a collaborative community for students of all skill levels.

## Leadership

CAIR leadership includes: President Kento Hopkins, Vice President Eric Fackelman, Treasurer Ethan Johnson, and Secretary Ajay Sudhir. Additional officers include Brian, Saige, Batman, Rafan, Smit, and Summer.

## Offerings

CAIR offers hands-on AI experience through student-led projects, the CAIR Lab for collaboration, general body meetings, workshops, hackathons, competitions, and interdisciplinary opportunities. All experience levels and majors are welcome.

## Membership Requirements

Official CAIR membership requires submitting the membership application at https://forms.gle/NGXKqq3JGWw5B9QE8. Membership is required to join or start CAIR projects, take on leadership roles, access the CAIR Lab regularly, use shared resources, and receive recognition and priority access to limited opportunities. Membership is not required to attend CAIR events open to the public.
