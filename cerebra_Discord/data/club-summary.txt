Charlotte AI Research (CAIR)
Overview & Mission


Charlotte AI Research (CAIR) is a student-led organization at UNC Charlotte dedicated to hands-on projects, applied research, and
skill-building in artificial intelligence.​



Mission: Build a supportive community of curious learners who apply AI responsibly and creatively, making AI more accessible,
relevant, and impactful for both members and the broader community.​



Focus areas: Machine Learning, Computer Vision, Robotics, Natural Language Processing, Brain–Computer Interfaces, Educational
Technology.​



Approach: Learn-by-doing through semester-long projects, collaborative labs, outreach events, paper reading groups, and partnerships
with industry/academia.​

Policies & Governance


Membership: Open to all students, faculty, staff, alumni. Voting rights reserved for enrolled students.​



Elections: Runoff ballot system; President & VP limited to one term.​



Officer Removal/Impeachment: Requires 4/5 vote of members.​



Amendments: Proposed by any member, ratified by Exec + member vote.​



Advisors: Faculty/staff advisors provide guidance and continuity.​



Meetings: At least 12 per year + exec board planning meetings.​



Annual Report: Required at end of year (activities, finances, inventory).​

Strategic Positioning


Computer Science is the most popular major at UNC Charlotte. Within CS, AI, gaming, and robotics is the most popular concentration.​



This makes AI central to the university’s identity: attracting new students, securing research funding, and building external partnerships.​



A strong AI student organization (CAIR) enhances this by:​


Giving students a community of practice outside the classroom.​



Providing hands-on AI experiences that supplement coursework.​



Helping students navigate unintuitive opportunities like undergraduate research, labs, and internships.​



Building cross-college collaboration (Engineering, Education, Business, Data Science, etc.).​

Institutional Momentum


UNC Charlotte recently achieved R1 status (top-tier research classification). With this comes pressure to grow cutting-edge research
areas, including AI.​



The Charlotte AI Institute for Research positions UNC Charlotte as a regional AI hub, but students need entry points. CAIR can serve
as the bridge between the Institute and undergraduates:​


Making research approachable.​



Onboarding students early into labs.​



Building continuity from student projects → faculty labs → industry roles.​

Regional & Industry Ties


Charlotte is a major banking and tech hub with partners like Bank of America, Wells Fargo, Ally, Truist, TIAA, Lowe’s Tech Hub, and
numerous startups.​



Industry demand for AI talent is growing rapidly — by 2030, AI will impact ~40% of working hours (WEF data, cited in CAIR interest slides).​



CAIR serves as a pipeline of talent for regional employers by:​


Equipping students with portfolio-ready projects.​



Encouraging internship prep and resume-building through applied AI work.​



Hosting CAIR Day/AI Day showcases to connect students and industry.​

Differentiators


Unlike traditional academic units, CAIR is student-led and interdisciplinary: it blends research, engineering, and community impact.​



Students learn both technical and leadership skills (project management, outreach, marketing, event planning).​



CAIR fills the gap between research excellence (AI Institute, labs) and student experience (clubs, internships, peer mentorship).​

Why It Matters


For UNC Charlotte: CAIR strengthens AI reputation, helps recruit students, and provides evidence of student engagement at an R1
institution.​



For students: CAIR offers clarity in a complex ecosystem, showing clear paths from freshman → projects → research → internships
→ careers/grad school.​



For Charlotte’s industry: CAIR is a direct talent funnel, giving employers motivated, skilled students who already have project experience.
