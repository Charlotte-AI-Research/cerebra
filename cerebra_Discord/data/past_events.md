# CAIR Past Events

## Fall 2024

In Fall 2024, Charlotte AI Research (CAIR) hosted its semester Interest Meeting. Members learned about CAIR's mission, upcoming projects, and how to get involved. This event introduced new students to the CAIR lab, project teams, and leadership opportunities.

During Fall 2024, CAIR held multiple General Body Meetings. Topics included using a CS degree effectively, starting research, meeting faculty, and hands-on workshops with large language models. These meetings helped students build technical and academic skills.

CAIR organized an outdoor social event in Fall 2024. A planned hike to Linville Gorge was rescheduled due to weather, and members instead attended a group hike at Morrow Mountain.

CAIR hosted an end-of-semester potluck in Fall 2024. Members gathered to celebrate the end of the term, share food, and build community.

Throughout Fall 2024, CAIR held recurring CAIR Reads sessions, where students discussed AI research papers as a group. These meetings helped members build research literacy and critical thinking skills.

## Spring 2025

At the start of Spring 2025, CAIR participated in the university's GoldRush Student Showcase by hosting a booth. CAIR introduced new students to projects, leadership opportunities, and the CAIR lab.

CAIR hosted two Spring 2025 Interest Meetings to present projects, explain the membership process, and welcome returning and new students.

In Spring 2025, CAIR held several General Body Meetings covering elections, involvement pathways, project updates, professional development, lab changes, and creative competitions such as CAIR merchandise design.

Hack with CAIR was a week-long AI hackathon hosted during Spring 2025. Students formed teams, built machine learning projects, and presented their work. It included an opening ceremony, project development time, and final submissions.

Throughout Spring 2025, CAIR held open Executive Board Meetings. These meetings allowed members to observe planning, leadership decisions, and organizational strategy.

During Spring 2025, CAIR hosted a club-wide meeting to discuss the direction of the organization for the upcoming academic year. Members gave input on vision, goals, and improvements.

## Fall 2025

CAIR began Fall 2025 with its main Interest Meeting in Woodward Hall. Students learned about CAIR's mission, upcoming events, and active projects.

General Body Meetings during Fall 2025 included workshops on undergraduate research, LLM internals, professional development, and several community-building sessions.

CAIR hosted a Tech Debate Night in Fall 2025, where members discussed major technological and AI-related issues in a structured debate format.

In Fall 2025, CAIR organized an AI Startup Founder Panel featuring local entrepreneurs. Students learned about startup journeys, industry experience, and AI innovation.

CAIR held a Trivia Night and team-building event in Fall 2025 to prepare students for the CCI Startup Hackathon weekend.

CAIR hosted a professional development workshop featuring Rick Scot, a Global CISO, who spoke about cybersecurity, leadership, and professional growth.

AI Day, held in Fall 2025, was CAIR's large end-of-semester showcase. Students presented their AI projects, gave demos, and engaged with the community. The event featured food, outreach, and networking.
